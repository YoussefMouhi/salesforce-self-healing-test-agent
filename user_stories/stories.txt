As a buyer, I want to search for a product by name, so that I can quickly find what I'm looking for without scrolling through the full catalog.
As a buyer, I want to add a product to my cart, so that I can purchase it later at checkout.

As a buyer, I want to change the quantity of an item already in my cart, so that I can adjust my order before checking out.
As a buyer, I want to remove an item from my cart, so that I don't purchase something I no longer want.
As a buyer, I want to review my cart and submit my order at checkout, so that my purchase is finalized.
